\section{Mentorship}
\cventry{2024-Present}{Undergraduate Mentee}{Ashleigh Smith}{Chapel Hill, NC}{Anomalous Diffusion}{Ashleigh created a PAAm gel protocol to meet complex experimental requirements for a poroelastic substrate in an isolated chamber. As she has matured as a researcher, this work has segued into a more autonomous project studying the wicking effect of poroelastic media.}
\cventry{2025-Present}{Undergraduate Mentee}{Mason Miller}{Chapel Hill, NC}{Anomalous Diffusion}{Mason designed a 3D printed strain control system and ran many strain experiments in poroelastic media. Mason is now working to characterize the rheologic properties of poroelastic media during strains via atomic force microscopy.}
