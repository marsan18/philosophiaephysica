\subsection{Masters Thesis}
\cvitem{}{\emph{Physiological Strains in Poroelastic Media Induce Asymmetric Transport}}
\cvitem{Advisor}{Richard Superfine}
\cvitem{Thesis Comittee}{Dr. Richard Superfine, Dr. Greg M. Forrest, Dr. David Hill, Dr. Amy Oldenburg, Dr. Kerry Bloom }
\cvitem{Description}{Tracked quantum dots undergoing dynamic diffusion in poroelastic media using LSFM with unprecedented temporal resolution. Live imaging during physiological strain reveals that large solute transport is asymmetric during dynamic strains, with advection yeilding significantly more forward angular anisotropy during the relaxation stroke than the compression stroke. These results suggest single-particle effects could explain the macroscopic solute concentration enhancements reported by studies of poroelastic media under dynamic strain.}
